How to read these metrics

Total stress is the average of central and peripheral stress. Central emphasizes relative load; peripheral also weights proximity to failure. These are estimates of workload, not measurements of nervous-system fatigue or recovery limits.

Session windows use 80–120% of the median session from the preceding 56 days; elevated is above 120%, much higher above 150%. Fewer than six prior sessions is provisional. Historical windows use only sessions before the workout being assessed. Missed weeks do not become zero-stress sessions.

Rolling totals are trailing 7- and 28-day durations, including each saved workout once. Gaps stay visible; the lines join recorded sessions and do not imply workouts occurred between them. Changing the history selector never changes today’s rolling totals or removes earlier data needed for a baseline.

Warm-ups are excluded. Unsupported or invalid sets are marked unscored. Historical estimates leave the original workout data untouched. This dashboard covers saved workouts; the active draft is shown on the workout log.
